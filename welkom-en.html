Safety first

  • Never your exact location. Others see at most an area of about 300 metres.
  • You can always go invisible, free, with one button.
  • 18+ only. An ID check will be added before the official launch.
  • Report and block with one tap.
  • Not while driving. If you move too fast, the screen locks, unless you're a passenger.
  • Socials only with your permission. Your Instagram and Snapchat stay hidden until you share them.
  • Meet in a public place and tell someone where you are.

Frequently asked questions

Can someone see exactly where I am?

No. Others see at most an area of about 300 metres, or the place where you check in yourself. With the invisible button they see nothing.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Galaxy works in your phone's browser. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. A Play Store version is being prepared.

Is it a game?

No. It looks playful because of the map and your avatar, but the goal is meeting real people in real places.

Why 18+ only?

Galaxy is made for dating and meeting new people. That's for adults. Minors are refused and removed.

How does the map know where it's busy?

For now it's an estimate based on the kind of place and the time. Once there are members, Galaxy counts how many people are in each area, without showing who is where.

Does it cost money?

No. The beta is completely free. Later there will be plans for extras; the basics stay free.

Does it work in my city?

The map works everywhere in the world. Meeting other members works where enough people use Galaxy. Invite your friends to get your city going.

Honest about where we are

Galaxy is being built. The map, crowds, your avatar, check-ins, group dates, waving and chatting after a match work in the beta. ID age checks, calling, video calls and payments come later. Until then everything is free.
