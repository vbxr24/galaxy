3. Your location

Your exact location never leaves your device. The app first rounds it to a zone of about 300 metres and only sends that zone. Others only see who is in a zone after 15 minutes. Live crowds only show a number, and only from 3 members in a zone. If you go invisible, your zone is deleted from the server. The app measures your speed via GPS to lock the screen while driving; that speed is not stored. A check-in at a place is kept on the server for up to 2 hours to count members.

4. Feature by feature

Photo, video and voice

Only you and your matches see a real photo. Your video intro is seen by your matches or everyone nearby, as you choose. Your voice intro can be heard by logged-in members who haven't blocked you. You can delete them whenever you want.

Feed

A story is visible for 24 hours to your matches and people in your wider area (± 25 km, based on your rounded zone) and is deleted afterwards. Answers to the question of the night are deleted after 14 days. Bars can post deals; they don't see who viewed them.

Chat

Messages, photos and voice messages are only visible to you and your match, via temporary links. "Read" and "typing" are only shown in a conversation with a match.

Automatic nudity check

Before a photo or video is uploaded, your own device checks it for nudity with the open-source model NSFWJS. This happens entirely on your device. Clear nudity is refused; doubtful cases are first reviewed by a human. Texts with links, phone numbers, email addresses or offensive words are refused. Anything reported twice is hidden until a human reviews it.

Age check (18+)

Through our processor Didit you take a short selfie; Didit estimates your age and only asks for an ID document if in doubt. Galaxy only receives the result (approved or not, estimated age), never your selfie or document. This is an automated decision: if you disagree, email us and a human will review it.

Verification (selfie, optional)

Your selfie with a requested gesture is only viewed by the Galaxy administrator and deleted after the decision. We only keep that you are verified and when.

Dates, safe home and feedback

If you plan a date, we store what you both fill in; only the two of you see it. Weather comes from Open-Meteo using only the coordinates of the place. A safe-home check stores the time and the chosen match for up to 3 days, without location. Feedback after a date ("did you feel safe?") is never shown to the other person; a "no" goes confidentially to the Galaxy team.

Badges, streaks, invites, Galaxy nights

Badges are calculated from what you already do in Galaxy; others see which badges you have, not the details. For invites we store who invited whom. Bars only see totals for Galaxy nights, never names.

Notifications (push)

Off by default. A notification never contains message content or location. "Someone nearby" never says who or where; to avoid duplicates we keep for 3 days who got a notification about whom.

Socials and music

Your Instagram and Snapchat names are visible to no one by default. If you paste a Spotify link, your profile loads a Spotify player.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile: as long as your account exists. After deletion we erase everything within 30 days.
  • Zones: no longer shown after 2 hours. Check-ins at places: 2 hours. Stories: visible for 24 hours, then 2 more days invisible in the database. Answers: 14 days. Bar deals: 14 days.
  • Verification selfie: until the decision. Safe-home checks: 3 days.
  • Reports and moderation: up to 1 year after handling, or longer if the law or an investigation requires it.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, erase, restrict or port your data, object, and withdraw any consent at any time. Much of this works instantly in the app: Download my data and Delete account in your profile. For anything else, email us; we reply within one month. You can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be) or the supervisory authority in your own country. If you live outside the EU (for example in the UK, the US or elsewhere), we give you the same rights. We do not sell or "share" personal data for advertising.

8. Minors

Galaxy is for 18+ only. We delete accounts of anyone found to be younger, and report sexual content involving minors to the authorities.

9. Storage on your device and cookies

Galaxy uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party analytics, so there is no cookie banner. The app does store what it needs in your own browser storage (your settings, language, your figure, your login key and an offline copy of the app). If you clear the site data, it's gone.

10. Security

Connections are encrypted. Files are kept in protected storage and only shown via temporary links. Admin access is limited to the Galaxy administrator.
